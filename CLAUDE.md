# alondras-cleaning

## Goal

Rebuild and own the marketing site for **Alondra's Cleaning Services LLC** — a local cleaning business in the Methow Valley. Replaces the old `alondrascleaningllc.com` site, which was on a third-party CRM template (interscopemediacrm) that the owner is canceling.

The site is a marketing surface, not a booking platform. The job: visitor → text/call → human conversation. Bookings happen by phone.

## File map

- `public/` — what Cloudflare Pages serves (HTML, CSS, JS, images)
  - `index.html`, `about.html`, `services.html`, `gallery.html`, `contact.html` — generated by `build.py`
  - `styles.css`, `script.js`
  - `images/` — self-hosted assets (logo, favicon, 9 curated job photos)
- `build.py` — generator. Run `python3 build.py` to regenerate `public/*.html`
- `BACKLOG.md` — warm / quiet / composting. Open questions for the owner at top
- `VISION.md` — upstream sketch of what the site could become; items earn their way into BACKLOG
- `NOTICES.md` — outside-eye observations parked for later
- `THREAD.jsonl` — append-only session log
- `.gitignore` — strict; secrets, editor folders, .claude/

Anything outside `public/` is repo-only and never deploys.

## Decisions (don't relitigate)

- **Static site, no backend.** No booking system, no customer accounts. Bookings happen by text or call.
- **Text-first contact.** `sms:` links are the primary CTAs throughout. Phone secondary, email tertiary, form last.
- **Self-hosted assets.** Originally pulled from CRM firebase URLs; cached locally 2026-05-05 before owner cancels the CRM.
- **English customer-facing.** Her customers speak English. Site stays English. (See VISION for bilingual signal.)
- **Editorial visual direction.** Cream + sage + clay palette, Fraunces serif, centered logo. Replaces the brown+cyan CRM template.
- **Cloudflare Pages auto-deploy from `main`.** Build output dir is `public/`. Build command empty (HTML is committed).
- **Repo is `samheller/alondras-cleaning` (public).** Will transfer to owner's GitHub eventually.
- **Domain: `alondrascleaning.com`** (new). Old `alondrascleaningllc.com` is decommissioning.

## Conventions

- One concrete thing per response, not a questionnaire.
- Append to `THREAD.jsonl` when something lands, shifts, or opens. In the moment, not at the end.
- Backlog items earn their way to **warm** by signal (owner says it, real flow surfaces it, customer asks). Not by interestingness.
- Public-facing copy: plain, valley-rooted, no jargon, no marketing fluff.
- Photos: only owner's own job photos. No stock. Skip toilets/befores/dirty unless the visual story is "we handle the gross stuff" (it isn't).
- Commit messages capture what was said + what shifted. Brief is fine.
- Before each session: read `BACKLOG.md`, tail `THREAD.jsonl`.
- After each session: update both, commit.

## Operator context

- **Alondra speaks Spanish natively; her customers speak English.** This shapes everything we write:
  - Customer-facing copy stays English.
  - Anything *she* might read or edit should be plain language, low-jargon, ideally translatable. If a handoff doc gets written for her, write it in Spanish too — don't make her translate engineer-speak.
  - Avoid idioms in any voice she might adopt as her own.
  - Keep content structure swap-friendly (no prose welded into HTML in ways that block a future bilingual variant).
- **Non-technical owner.** She should never have to clone a repo or run a command. Edits should happen via someone else (Sam, or eventually a CMS or Decap-style web editor) — not her at the terminal.
- **Mobile sauna she's not.** This is a service business with seasonal rhythm but no schedule UI to own. The site is more like a brochure than an app.
- **The old CRM is leaving.** As of 2026-05-05 we cached all 11 assets locally; site is fully decoupled. Old domain already returns DNS failure.
- **Pricing is by quote.** No prices on the site. Confirm with her before adding.
